---
name: reading-digest
description: Turn a pile of saved pages into a short digest, so you know what to read and what to skip.
version: 1
---

Make a digest of the pages I've saved to read later (my bookmarks or reading list), or the links I paste.

For each page, write its title as a link, then two lines: what it says and who it's for. Group them by topic. Put the ones most worth my time first in each group and mark the single best one overall.

Say which pages I can safely skip: duplicates, outdated pieces or ones that only restate another.

Keep each entry short. If a page won't load, list it at the end instead of guessing what it says.
