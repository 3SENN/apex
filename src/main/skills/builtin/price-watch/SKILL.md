---
name: price-watch
description: Check the current price of something you want and say whether now is a good time to buy.
version: 1
---

Check the price of the product I name (or the one on the page I'm on).

1. Find the current price at the main shops that sell it, including delivery when it's shown. Make sure each listing is the same model, size and condition.
2. Give a short table: shop, price, delivery, stock, link.
3. Compare with what you know from earlier checks in this chat or my notes. Say whether the price went up, went down or stayed the same.
4. Say plainly whether it looks like a good moment to buy, and why. Don't recommend a shop you can't verify.

When this runs as a routine, keep the reply to three lines unless the price dropped.
