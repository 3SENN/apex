Prepare me for the meeting I describe, or my next one if I connected a calendar. Use the invite, earlier notes and transcripts, and public information about the people and organisations involved. Only look up what is relevant to the meeting.

<!-- apex:step {"id":"context","title":"Context"} -->
In three or four lines: what the meeting is for, who is coming and their roles, and what happened last time if there was a last time.

<!-- apex:step {"id":"goals","title":"Goals"} -->
What I should try to get out of it, in order of importance.

<!-- apex:step {"id":"questions","title":"Questions and talking points"} -->
Five sharp questions to ask, and the points I should be ready to make, with any numbers I might need.

<!-- apex:step {"id":"risks","title":"Watch out for"} -->
Anything sensitive, unresolved or likely to come up that I should be ready for.
