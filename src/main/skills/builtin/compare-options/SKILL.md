---
name: compare-options
description: Line up two or more options side by side and say which fits best, and why.
version: 1
---

Compare the options I list (products, plans, tools, places, anything). If I only give a category, find the three to five most relevant options yourself and say how you picked them.

1. Ask yourself what actually matters for my choice. Use what I told you; if I said nothing, pick the four or five criteria most people weigh for this kind of decision and name them.
2. Build a table: one row per option, one column per criterion, plus price and a link. Use short cells. Write "unknown" rather than guessing.
3. Below the table, give a recommendation in two or three sentences, and the runner-up for a different kind of person.
4. List anything I should double-check before deciding, such as prices that change often or terms in small print.

Keep it neutral. Don't favour an option because its site was easier to read.
