# Meetings

- {brand} can transcribe a meeting from your microphone and the system audio, on this Mac. Start it from the Meetings page (apex://meetings) or when a calendar event begins.
- While recording, a small live dot shows in the sidebar's Meetings row. Stop any time; the transcript is saved locally.
- After a meeting, ask the companion for a summary, action items or a follow-up email. The transcript stays attached to the meeting.
- Calendar access is optional and read-only; it only helps {brand} offer to record at the right moment.
- Let people know when you record a meeting, and follow the rules where you are.
