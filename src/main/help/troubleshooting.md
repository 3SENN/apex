# Troubleshooting

- The companion doesn't answer: check Settings › Model and key. The API key must be valid, and any other engine you chose must be set up and signed in.
- A page won't load: try reloading, check the network, and look at the site's permissions from the address bar.
- The companion can't act on a page: {brand}'s own pages (apex://) and some protected sites are off limits to it. Open the page in a normal tab.
- A connector fails: open apex://connectors, check its health, and reconnect or check its logs.
- {brand} feels slow: close tabs you don't need, or turn off pages you don't use from the sidebar. Diagnostics in Settings can save a report to share.
- Keep {brand} in the Dock: while it's open, right-click its Dock icon and choose Options › Keep in Dock. {brand} never changes the Dock for you.
