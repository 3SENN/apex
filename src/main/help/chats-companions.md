# Chats and companions

- Every chat belongs to one companion. A companion has its own look, name, standing instructions, model choice, skills and memory.
- Add companions from the companion card (click the picture) or from the preset gallery. Presets come with a personality and a few starter skills and routines. Companions can be hidden without losing their chats.
- All chats (atelier://chats) lists every chat with search and filters. Chats can be pinned, renamed, exported (text, Markdown, HTML or PDF) and moved to a project.
- While a chat runs you can keep typing; new messages wait in line. Esc or the stop button ends a run. Steps already taken are not undone.
- Approvals: the companion shows a card before consequential actions. Settings › Agent controls whether it asks for everything or only for what matters.
- Private chats keep no history and no memory once closed.
- Imported conversations from other assistants open read-only; their banner offers to carry them on in a fresh chat with a summary or the transcript as context.
