# Meet your companion

Your companion lives in {brand} with you. It can read the page you're on, click and type in your tabs, search the web, and keep notes about what you like. You stay in charge the whole time: you can watch every step, stop it, and it asks before doing anything that matters.

## Asking for help

Press `⌘J` to open the side panel next to the page you're reading, or type into the box on a new tab. Ask the way you'd ask a friend who is good with computers:

- "Find three quiet cafés near the station that are open on Sunday."
- "Read this article and tell me the main argument in two sentences."
- "Fill in this form with my work address."

If something is unclear, your companion asks a short question first instead of guessing.

## Watching it work

While your companion works in a tab, the tab glows in its colour and its face shows a small working mark. You can keep browsing in another tab. If it needs your go-ahead, say before submitting a form, the chat shows a request you can allow or deny.

> Tip: click your companion's picture to open its card. That's where its skills, routines, files and memory live.

## Making it yours

Every companion is built from parts: a body, a head, hair, ears, a hat, glasses and a prop, plus a face and four colours. Open the card and choose **Customize look** to change any of them, or shuffle for a surprise. You can rename it, give it a personality, and write standing instructions it follows in every new chat.

You can have more than one companion, too. Each keeps its own chats, skills and memory, so one can help with work while another plans trips.
