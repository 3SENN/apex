# Chats and tabs

In {brand}, chats and tabs sit side by side. A chat can work on the page next to it, open new tabs of its own, and come back to you with an answer.

## Starting a chat

There are three easy ways in:

1. **A new tab.** Type a question into the box in the middle and press Enter. The tab turns into the chat.
2. **The side panel.** Press `⌘J` on any page to talk about what you're looking at. The page stays put on the left.
3. **The sidebar.** Choose **New chat** in the chats section, or press `⇧⌘E`.

## Keeping track

Every chat appears in the sidebar under its companion's face. The face shows how the chat is doing: working, waiting for you, finished, or stuck. Chats that finished while you were elsewhere stay marked until you look at them.

Open [All chats](apex://chats) to search everything you've asked, pin the ones you come back to, and archive the rest.

## Tabs the chat opens

When your companion opens pages for a task, they show up as tabs that belong to that chat. They glow while it's working in them. When the task is done you can keep them, or close them all at once.

> Tip: `⌘1` to `⌘9` jump to the rows in your sidebar, in the order you see them, chats included.
