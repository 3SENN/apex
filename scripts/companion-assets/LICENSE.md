# Atelier Companion Art: the recipes

The scripts in this folder generate the companion art in `src/renderer/public/companion/`: the character parts, rigs,
face layers, configuration and the preset companions. They are part of the Atelier Companion Art and are licensed under
the [Atelier Companion Art License](../../ART-LICENSE.md), not under the Mozilla Public License that covers Atelier's code.

The engine that loads and draws these files (`src/shared/companion.ts`, `src/shared/companionLogic.ts`,
`src/renderer/src/companion/` and `src/kit/`) is MPL-2.0. A fork brings designs of its own; see
[docs/companion-system.md › Bring your own designs](../../docs/companion-system.md#bring-your-own-designs).
