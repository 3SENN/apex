# Building on Atelier

Atelier is a browser, and it is also a platform. Its companion works in real tabs, its kit lets anyone build apps and
games that run inside it, and everything people make can travel to others as a pack. The code is open, so the platform
belongs to everyone who builds on it.

## The vision

**Personal AI should belong to people, not to platforms.** Atelier puts an AI companion in a browser you own: the code is
open, your data stays on your computer, and you choose the model it runs on.

**A platform gets stronger with everyone who builds on it.** Every pack, app, companion and connector someone makes
makes Atelier more useful for everyone else, and every contribution to the core makes the base better for all of them.
Together we can build something no single company can: a browser and an AI platform that belongs to the people who use
it and build on it.

**Open standards, no lock-in.** Atelier speaks the web, runs Chrome Web Store extensions and connects to any
[MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server. What you build for Atelier builds on standards you already know.

## For creators and organisations

- **Earn from what you make.** Packs, apps and companions for Atelier may be sold, including ones that use Atelier's companion
  art (see [ART-LICENSE.md](../ART-LICENSE.md)).
- **Bring your brand into Atelier.** A pack, app or world can carry your own character as its mascot, inside Atelier.
- **Run it in your organisation.** An organisation's policy can force-install, allow or block packs and hand them
  managed values and sign-in.

## The principles we build on

- **The person stays in control.** The companion asks before anything that matters, and it is never allowed to enter
  passwords or card details.
- **Private by design.** Atelier keeps your data on your computer and has no analytics; see [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
- **Safe to share.** Packs are signed, reviewed before every install and sandboxed.
- **Open by default.** The code is [MPL-2.0](../LICENSE), and Atelier builds on open standards.

## Start building

1. **Make something today:** ask your companion to build a skill, a routine or a small app with you, then share it as a
   pack ([packs.md](packs.md) › Sharing). To build by hand, start with the kit in [apps.md](apps.md#the-kit).
2. **Learn the core:** read [architecture.md](architecture.md) and the specs in [design/](design/).
3. **Contribute:** pick a [good first issue](https://github.com/3SENN/atelier/labels/good%20first%20issue) and read
   [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
4. **Join the community:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/sKUTVCYAZY) and
   [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/3SENN/atelier/discussions).
